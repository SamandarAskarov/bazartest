The Concept

Neo-Orientalism, reimagined.

BAZAAR is built around contrast — ancient references rendered through a contemporary lens.

Central Asian motifs meet gallery-grade architecture. Sculptural light, deep materials and an open kitchen turn dinner into an atmosphere you step inside.

It is not a themed room. It is a contemporary space that remembers where it stands.
